## The hardware difference

**Mainlines** (the toy-aisle cars) have a diecast metal body on a **plastic chassis**,
hard plastic wheels, and simple printed graphics. They're made in enormous quantities and
they're great toys — that's the point.

**Premium** lines have a **metal body AND metal chassis** ("metal/metal"), **Real Riders
rubber tires** with styled rims, far more detailed multi-layer tampo printing, and
collector card art. Pick one up and the weight difference alone tells you.

sub-series (Aero Styles, Japan Historics, Ronin Run, Vintage Racing Club, Modern Classics,
Power Trip), **Boulevard**, **Pop Culture** licensed sets, **Fast & Furious**, and the
**F1 gold-label** line.

## Which should you buy?

For a kid to play with: mainlines, no contest. For display, collecting, or holding value:
premium — a mint premium card keeps its worth in a way a played-with mainline can't, and
sought-after castings (LBWK, Pandem, RE Amemiya liveries) frequently trade above retail
once sold out.
