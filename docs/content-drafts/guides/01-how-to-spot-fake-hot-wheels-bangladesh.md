Counterfeit and "fantasy" Hot Wheels cards circulate in every market, and Bangladesh is no
exception — especially in marketplace listings where the price looks too good. Here's the
checklist we use ourselves when sourcing, so you can verify any card in a couple of
minutes.

## 1. Check the base

Flip the car. A genuine Hot Wheels base is stamped with the **Mattel name, a year, and a
country of manufacture** — Malaysia, Thailand or Indonesia for modern production. The text
is crisp and evenly moulded. Fakes commonly have shallow, blurry or missing base text, or
a base that doesn't match the casting at all.

## 2. Read the card printing

Genuine card art is sharp, colour-accurate and correctly spelled. Look closely at the
smallest text on the back — legal lines and barcodes. Counterfeit cards are usually
reprinted at lower resolution: fuzzy micro-text, washed-out colours, off-centre cutting.
Spelling mistakes are an instant fail.

## 3. Inspect the blister

Mattel's blisters are uniform, firmly glued, and shaped for the specific card layout. Be
suspicious of bubbles that are re-glued (lift marks at the edges), cloudy plastic on a
"new" card, or a car sitting loose in a bubble clearly moulded for a different model —
that's the classic sign of a swapped or re-carded piece.

## 4. Know what the wheels should be

Premium lines — Car Culture, Boulevard, Pop Culture, Fast & Furious, F1 — come with **Real
Riders rubber tires** and a **metal chassis**. If a "premium" card has hard plastic wheels
or a plastic base showing through, it's either a fake or a mainline swapped onto a premium
card. (Our guide to Premium vs Mainline covers the differences in detail.)

## 5. Price sanity-check

Premium Hot Wheels reach Bangladesh as low-quantity imports. If a Car Culture card is
offered at mainline money — a fraction of the ৳1,300+ it costs anywhere in the country —
the discount IS the warning. Compare against a trusted seller's live prices before
believing an outlier.

## 6. Buy from sellers who show you the actual piece

A legitimate seller photographs the exact unit you'll receive and will happily send more
photos of the base, card back and blister on request. At DiecastBD every listing is the
photographed piece, and we'll shoot any angle you ask for before you pay — cash on
delivery means you can also inspect at the door inside Dhaka.
