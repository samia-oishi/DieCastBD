## Why premium prices vary so much

Three things move the number in Bangladesh: **the series** (licensed Pop Culture costs
Mattel more to make, and you more to buy), **import quantity** (most premium castings
arrive in single-digit quantities), and **demand** (JDM liveries — LBWK, Pandem, RE
Amemiya — sell out fastest and rarely restock). A sold-out premium doesn't come back at
the old price, if it comes back at all.
