## What "Hot Wheels Premium" actually means

Hot Wheels Premium is Mattel's collector line — different hardware from the ৳300–400
mainlines you see in supermarkets. A premium casting has a **metal body on a metal
chassis** (mainlines use a plastic base), **Real Riders rubber tires** instead of hard
plastic wheels, and multi-layer tampo-printed liveries on collector card art. That's what
you're paying for, and it's why a premium card holds its value while a mainline usually
doesn't.

The premium umbrella covers the series we stock: **Car Culture** (Aero Styles, Japan
Historics, Ronin Run, Vintage Racing Club, Modern Classics, Power Trip), **Boulevard**,
**Pop Culture** (licensed sets like Cyberpunk 2077 and Stranger Things), **Fast &
Furious**, and the **Formula 1 gold-label** line.
