## What is MINI GT?

MINI GT is the 1:64 line from **TSM (True Scale Miniatures)** — a model maker, not a toy
company, and it shows. A MINI GT casting has finer panel detail, a full printed interior,
rubber tyres and liveries reproduced to scale-model standards. In the JDM scene
especially — Liberty Walk, Pandem and RE Amemiya collaborations — MINI GT is the benchmark
for what a 1:64 can look like.

Two packaging styles exist: the standard **colour box** and the **blister card**, which
many collectors prefer for display. Numbered releases (#1022, #1046, #1094, #1106…) are
one production run each — when a number sells through, that exact release is gone.
