## Hot Wheels price in Bangladesh

Hot Wheels covers two very different things, and the price gap between them is the
first thing worth understanding. **Mainline** cars — the standard 1:64 castings —
run from about **৳400 to ৳950** here. **Premium** cars, the collector line, run from
about **৳850 to ৳6,600** depending on series and rarity.

The live price list on this page always shows the current figure for every Hot

## What you're paying for

A **mainline** car has a diecast metal body on a plastic chassis with plastic
wheels. Great to play with, easy to collect, and the natural place to start.

A **premium** car has a metal body *and* metal chassis, **Real Riders rubber
tires**, multi-layer tampo printing and collector card art. That's where the
weight, the detail and the resale value come from — and it's most of what we
stock, because it's what collectors come back for.

Premium covers **Car Culture** (Aero Styles, Japan Historics, Ronin Run, Vintage
Racing Club, Modern Classics, Power Trip), **Boulevard**, **Pop Culture** licensed
sets, **Fast & Furious**, **Team Transport** and the **Formula 1** gold-label line.

## Why premium costs more here

Premium castings reach Bangladesh in small quantities, and the ones collectors
chase hardest — Liberty Walk, Pandem and RE Amemiya liveries especially — don't
restock once a run sells out. A sold-out premium rarely returns at the old price,
if it returns at all. Mainline is produced in far larger numbers, which is exactly
why it stays affordable.
