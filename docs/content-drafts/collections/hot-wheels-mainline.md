## What you get in a mainline car

A mainline Hot Wheels has a **diecast metal body on a plastic chassis**, plastic wheels,
and printed graphics on the standard blister card. That build is why they cost a fraction
of premium cards — and it's exactly the right thing if you want a car to play with, gift,
or start collecting cheaply without worrying about every scratch.

Mainline releases are grouped into themed series each year — HW Exotics, J-Imports, Muscle
Mania, HW Race Team, Baja Blazers and more — so there's genuine variety rather than the
same handful of castings repeated.

## Treasure Hunts: the reason people hunt mainlines

Every mainline case hides chase cars. A **Treasure Hunt (TH)** carries a small flame logo
on the card and a low-key TH marking on the car. A **Super Treasure Hunt (STH)** goes
further — Spectraflame paint and Real Riders rubber tires, hidden in the same ordinary
cardboard. They cost the same as any mainline at retail, and finding one is the closest
thing this hobby has to a lottery ticket.

That's what makes mainline collecting different from premium: you're not just buying a
car, you're checking every card.
