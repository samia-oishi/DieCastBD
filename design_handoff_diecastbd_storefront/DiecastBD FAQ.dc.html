Straight answers.

The questions collectors actually ask — no fine print runaround.

Are your models 100% authentic?
Yes — every piece is sourced directly and inspected before it's listed. No replicas, no customs passed off as originals, ever. If we wouldn't put it on our own shelf, it doesn't get listed.
How long does delivery take?
Inside Dhaka: 24–48 hours. Everywhere else in Bangladesh: 2–4 days, tracked from dispatch to doorstep. You'll get the tracking link as soon as it ships.
Which payment methods do you accept?
Cash on Delivery anywhere in Bangladesh, bKash Send Money, and BanglaQR from any bank app. For bKash you just enter your Transaction ID at checkout.
How do you pack cards and boxes?
Collector-grade: card protectors on carded pieces, double-boxing, corner protection, and void fill. What you see online is what arrives — mint, with sharp corners.
Do sold-out premium pieces come back?
Usually not — premium castings reach Bangladesh in one import batch and are retired when they sell through. Hit "Notify me" on a sold-out piece and you'll be first to know if a restock ever happens.
Can I return or exchange an order?
Yes — 7 days for unopened items or anything that arrives not as described. Full details in the Refund Policy; message us with your order ID and we'll sort it fast.

Message us — we usually reply within the hour.
