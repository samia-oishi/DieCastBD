THE SHORT VERSION
· 7 days to return unopened items · Wrong or damaged item — free replacement or full refund · Refunds land in your bKash within 5–7 business days

Eligibility

You can return any item within 7 days of delivery if it's unopened and in the condition it arrived — seals intact, blister uncut, card unbent. Contact us with your order ID to start.

Damaged or not as described

If your order arrives damaged or doesn't match the listing, report it within 48 hours of delivery — an unboxing photo or video helps us resolve it in one message. You choose: a free replacement (stock permitting) or a full refund including shipping.

How refunds are paid

bKash and BanglaQR payments are refunded to the same account within 5–7 business days of approval. COD orders are refunded by bKash Send Money to the number on your order.

What can't be returned

Opened blisters, cut cards, and items damaged after delivery aren't eligible — condition is everything in this hobby, and we can't resell an opened piece as mint.

Cancellations

You can cancel any order free of charge before it's dispatched — from My Orders or by messaging us. Prepaid amounts are refunded in full.

Need a return started? Send your order ID — we'll handle the rest.
